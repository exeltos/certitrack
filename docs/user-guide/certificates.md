# Managing certificates

## Adding a certificate

From the Certificates page, choose **Add certificate**, fill in the
certificate type, issue and expiry dates, and upload the certificate file
(PDF). You can add optional notes for internal reference.

## Certificate status

CertiTrack tracks each certificate's status automatically based on its
expiry date:

- **Active** — not expiring soon
- **Soon** — within the warning window (default: 30 days; configurable in
  notification preferences)
- **Expired** — past its expiry date

## Visibility

Each certificate can be marked as either:

- **Private** — visible only within your organization
- **Shared with partners** — visible to organizations you have an active
  partner relationship with

Certificate files are always served through short-lived, signed links —
partners never get a permanent public URL to your documents.

## Editing or removing a certificate

Editing a certificate updates its record and is recorded in your
organization's audit log. Removing a certificate is a soft delete: it stops
appearing in your active list but its history is preserved for audit
purposes.

## Bulk actions

Select multiple certificates using the checkboxes to apply status filters or
bulk actions (see the "select" toolbar that appears once you select at least
one certificate).
