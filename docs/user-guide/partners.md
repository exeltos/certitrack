# Partner relationships

## Connecting with a partner organization

Search for a partner organization by its VAT/AFM number or contact email
from the Partners page, and send a connection request. The other
organization will see it as a pending invitation and can accept or decline.

## What a connection gives you

Once a relationship is active, you can see any certificates the partner has
marked as "shared with partners" (and they can see yours, on the same
basis). Private certificates on either side stay private.

## Ending a relationship

- **Cancel a pending request**: removes the request entirely — nothing was
  ever shared, so there's nothing left over.
- **End an active relationship**: stops future certificate visibility
  between the two organizations. Historical audit records of the
  relationship are kept.

You can send a new request to the same organization again later, even after
a previous request was cancelled or a relationship was ended.
