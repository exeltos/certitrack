# Notifications

## Expiry reminders

CertiTrack sends reminders as certificates approach their expiry date. By
default, reminders go out at 60, 30, 15, 7, and 1 days before expiry — you
can customize which of these windows apply from your notification
preferences.

## Notification channels

- **In-app**: shown in the notification center within CertiTrack.
- **Email**: sent to your account email, if enabled.

You can toggle each channel independently, and separately control whether
you're notified about:
- Certificate expiry
- Partner relationship changes (new requests, acceptances, endings)
- Certificate changes made by teammates in your organization

## Delivery

Emails are queued and sent via a scheduled background process, typically
within a few minutes of the triggering event. If you're not receiving
expected emails, check your spam folder first, then contact support.
