# Getting started

## Creating your organization

1. Go to the registration page and choose the account type that matches your
   role — you'll register once as an organization, and can connect with
   partner organizations afterward rather than registering separately as a
   "supplier" or "company."
2. Provide your organization's legal name and VAT/AFM number — this is used
   to verify your organization and to let partner organizations find and
   connect with you.
3. Confirm your email address. You can't sign in until this is done.

## First login

After confirming your email, sign in with your email and password. If your
organization has enabled two-factor authentication (see
[Account security](./account-security.md)), you'll be asked for a 6-digit
code from your authenticator app after your password.

## Inviting teammates

From your organization's settings, you can invite additional users under
your organization with different roles:

- **Owner** — full control, including closing the account
- **Admin** — manage certificates, partners, and most settings
- **Member** — day-to-day certificate management without account-level settings
